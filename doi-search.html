About NanoBriefs

NanoBriefs turns dense nanoelectronics papers into structured, source-traceable research briefs. Every card is AI-assisted and human-reviewed, with explicit links to the original paper.

Our goal is to help researchers find and compare device metrics (mobility, on/off ratio, subthreshold swing) without reading every paper end-to-end.

Free to browse. More features coming in Phase 1.
